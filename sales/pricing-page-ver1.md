# Fairmake pricing (ver 1)

18 Nov 2026. First version, written for the site build. Kurt approved using these proposals.

The software is free (MIT). You pay a fixed price for the setup: we install the agents on your computer, map your exports (NetSuite, SAP Business One, Epicor, Infor, Acumatica, Fishbowl, JobBOSS, Katana, MRPeasy or QuickBooks Enterprise reports, your MES, CMMS or QMS exports, and your EDI 850, 810 and 856 files), set your tolerances, specs and rules, run the first reports with you and train one person on your team.

Prices sit with the other Fair Agents families. The retail and EDI pack costs more because each trading partner's files and compliance guide have to be mapped.

Above the limits: $2,500 per additional plant, or $1,200 per additional client business.

## Support after setup

- $200 a month for one pack
- $450 a month for the whole plant
- $300 a month per plant for multi-plant setups
- $1,200 a month for a partner rollout

Support covers changes in your exports (an ERP upgrade that renames columns, a new MES), setting changes you ask for (new tolerances, a new trading partner guide, new specs), new versions of the agents and a monthly check-in call.

## Included and not included

Included: installation on your computer, mapping your exports, your settings (tolerances, targets, dispute windows and fines from your trading partner guides, material specs, tariff code list, maintenance and calibration intervals), the first run of every agent in the package with you, one training session, the first reports.

Not included: posting to the ERP, paying or short-paying suppliers, shipping, changing standards, reorder points or schedules, releasing holds, transmitting EDI, filing with customs or any agency, or messaging customers and suppliers (the agents never do these; a person on your team does). Legal, tax, customs, safety or accounting advice; the agents do not classify goods or decide whether an injury is recordable. Logging in to your ERP, MES, EDI network, bank or customer portals.

Your files and reports stay on your computer. Employee and owner names are masked in anything the agents send to an AI model.
