# Fairmake Job Margins fixtures

Fictional jobs for fictional customers, in US dollars; estimator names are made up.

- `jobs.csv`: two jobs that lost money with large overruns (J-5002, J-5009), an overrun under target (J-5003), a closed job invoiced below its quote (J-5004), a job with no quote (J-5006), one under target (J-5007) and clean jobs.
- `settings.json`, `settings-strict.json`: default and stricter targets.
- `exports/jobboss-job-summary.csv`: JobBOSS-style job cost summary columns. Written from knowledge of the export, not checked against a live system.
