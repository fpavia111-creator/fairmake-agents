# Fairmake Overhead Absorption fixtures

Fictional work centers at a fictional fabrication plant, October and November 2026, in US dollars.

- `hours.csv`, `rates.csv`, `actuals.csv`: CNC is under-absorbed and ran well below budget hours, WELD and INSP are in line, PAINT is over-absorbed, ASSY has hours and no rate, SAW has a rate and hours but no actual spend.
- `settings.json`, `settings-wide.json`: default and wider tolerances.
- `exports/jobboss-labor-by-wc.csv`: hours with JobBOSS-style column names. Written from knowledge of the export, not checked against a live system.
