# Fairmake Capacity Load fixtures

Fictional open operations and work center calendars, loaded from 2026-11-16.

- `operations.csv`, `calendar.csv`: CNC overloaded in its first week and near capacity in the third; WELD with a past-due operation; PAINT and ASSY with room (one ASSY operation is beyond the horizon); SAW with no calendar.
- `settings.json`, `settings-overtime.json`: default limits, and a shop that plans overtime.
- `exports/infor-job-operations.csv`: Infor CloudSuite Industrial style job operations. Written from knowledge of the export, not checked against a live system.
