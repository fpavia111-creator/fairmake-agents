# Fairmake Supplier Scorecards fixtures

Fictional receipts from fictional suppliers.

- `receipts.csv`: a bar supplier late on one of three receipts; a foundry with rejects far above target; a fastener supplier invoicing above PO price; a steel supplier with only two receipts; a seal supplier with a clean record.
- `settings.json`, `settings-quality.json`: default weights and targets, and a quality-heavy scorecard with two days of grace.
- `exports/acumatica-receipts.csv`: Acumatica-style receipt columns. Written from knowledge of the export, not checked against a live system.
